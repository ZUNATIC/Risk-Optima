Enterprise Asset Criticality Framework

RiskOptima is built upon industry-recognized risk management standards, drawing directly from NIST SP 800-30 (Guide for Conducting Risk Assessments) and ISO/IEC 27005 frameworks.

Mathematical Risk Model

Asset priority is computed using a multi-variable tactical equation reflecting operational pressure and architecture exposure:

Score = (Impact × 2) + (Likelihood × 1) + (RTO × 2) + (Exposure × 1)
  • Impact (Weight: 2): Financial, operational, and reputational damage if compromised (Scale 1-5).
  • Likelihood (Weight: 1): Threat actor probability and vulnerability frequency (Scale 1-5).
  • RTO Urgency (Weight: 2): Recovery Time Objective constraint; business downtime tolerance (Scale 1-5).
  • Exposure (Weight: 1): Network visibility (Internet-facing perimeter vs. isolated enclave) (Scale 1-5).
Tier Threshold Hierarchy

Asset scores map directly into operational response tiers to govern enterprise triage and patching cycles:

Tier 1 (Critical) Score ≥ 26 Immediate emergency containment.
Tier 2 (High) Score 20 – 25 High priority continuous monitoring.
Tier 3 (Medium) Score 14 – 19 Standard maintenance patching.
Tier 4-5 (Low/Info) Score < 14 Routine background audit cycle.
