ENTERPRISE ASSET CRITICALITY FRAMEWORK

AEGIS-CORE is built upon industry-recognized risk management standards, primarily drawing from NIST SP 800-30 (Guide for Conducting Risk Assessments) and ISO/IEC 27005 information security risk management controls.

Mathematical Risk & Criticality Formula

Unlike simplistic matrix models, AEGIS-CORE calculates asset priority using a multi-variable tactical equation designed to reflect real-world infrastructure pressure:

Score = (Impact × 2) + (Likelihood × 1) + (RTO × 2) + (Exposure × 1)
  • Impact (Weight: 2): Measures financial, operational, and reputational damage if the asset is compromised (Scale 1-5).
  • Likelihood (Weight: 1): Evaluates threat actor probability and existing vulnerability frequency (Scale 1-5).
  • RTO Urgency (Weight: 2): Recovery Time Objective constraint; how fast the organization collapses if offline (Scale 1-5).
  • Exposure (Weight: 1): Network visibility (e.g., Internet-facing perimeter vs. air-gapped internal enclave) (Scale 1-5).
Tier Threshold Hierarchy

Asset scores map directly into operational response tiers to govern enterprise triage and automated patching cycles:

Tier 1 (Critical) Score ≥ 26 Immediate emergency remediation required.
Tier 2 (High) Score 20 – 25 High priority containment and monitoring.
Tier 3 (Medium) Score 14 – 19 Standard patching schedule.
Tier 4-5 (Low/Info) Score < 14 Routine background maintenance.
